Features

Everything you need to learn deeply

Aura brings together science-backed study techniques in one beautiful, distraction-free app.

🧠

Spaced Repetition

Our algorithm serves up material exactly when you're about to forget it — maximizing retention with minimum effort.

⏱️

Deep Focus Mode

Pomodoro-based sessions with ambient soundscapes and smart break reminders tuned to your cognitive rhythm.

🃏

Smart Flashcards

Import from PDFs, notes, or type manually. Aura auto-generates cards and ranks them by your personal weak spots.

📈

Progress Analytics

Visual insights into study time, retention, and mastery across subjects — so you always know where to focus next.

🎯

Goal Tracking

Set exam dates, break down syllabi, and let Aura build a personalised daily study plan that keeps you on track.

🌙

Offline & Private

All your notes and progress live on your device. Study on a plane, subway, or anywhere — no internet needed.
