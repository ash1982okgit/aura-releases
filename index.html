Features

Everything you need to learn deeply

Aura brings together science-backed study techniques in one beautiful, distraction-free app.

📄

Smart Syllabus Extraction

Import from PDFs or type your syllabus manually. Aura auto-generates comprehensive lesson plans, study sprints, and goals.

✨

Rich Multi-Modal Lessons

Experience lessons generated dynamically by AI, complete with rich formatting, contextual illustrations, and integrated video search.

🔊

Neural Text-To-Speech

Listen to your lessons on the go using high-quality voices from Edge TTS, OpenAI, or ElevenLabs — fully customizable.

🏆

Streaks & Spark Points

Stay motivated with a gamified learning experience. Earn Spark Points for completing daily missions and climb the global leaderboard.

⚙️

Custom AI Prompts

Take control of your AI tutor. Edit the core instructions given to the AI for syllabus extraction, lesson writing, and quiz generation.

🌙

Private & Flexible

Study on your terms. Use Guest Mode for local-first studying without an account, or log in to sync your progress securely.
